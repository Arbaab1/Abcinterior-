Good to know

Common questions

How much does a project cost?

Every project is bespoke. After the first consultation we share a detailed estimate broken down by design fee, materials and execution — no hidden costs, ever.

How long does a full home design take?

Design and 3D visualization typically take 3–5 weeks; execution runs 4–8 months depending on size, materials and site conditions. You get a written timeline before we start.

Do you work outside Mumbai, Goa and Delhi?

Yes. We take select projects across India and handle coordination remotely with scheduled on-site visits by our execution team.

What does “turnkey” mean?

One team handles everything — drawings, 3D, materials, civil work, furniture and final styling. You receive a finished, ready-to-live space with a single point of contact.

Can I see my home in 3D before construction?

Absolutely. Photoreal stills, video fly-throughs and interactive Unreal Engine walkthroughs are included in every full-scope project, so you approve every detail before work begins.
